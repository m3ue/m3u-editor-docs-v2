M3U Editor works with any IPTV player, and that's not changing. But a general-purpose player only sees what the Xtream API can describe, and that leaves out a lot of what M3U Editor can do. M3U TV is a player made for M3U Editor. Version 1.0, rebuilt from the ground up in Flutter, shipped on July 2, and the 1.0 series that followed filled it out quickly.

<!-- truncate -->

## One app, everywhere

M3U TV runs on Android TV and Google TV, Apple TV, Android and iOS phones and tablets, Mac, Windows, and Linux. It's built for a TV remote first: everything works with a D-pad, and it scales up for a screen across the room. On a phone or computer it uses touch and the mouse like any other app.

It's free and open source, on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.sparkison.tv), the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9P2PBHQ4XZ1L), TestFlight for Apple devices, and [GitHub](https://github.com/m3ue/m3u-tv/releases). See [M3U TV](/docs/m3u-tv/overview).

![A movie page in M3U TV](/img/tv/tv4.webp)

## What it does

- **Live TV** with a programme guide, favorites, and catch-up for channels that have it.
- **Movies and series** with artwork, cast, and details from your library and TMDB.
- **Continue Watching** that follows you between devices. Stop on your phone and resume on the TV. See [Continue Watching](/docs/m3u-tv/continue-watching).
- **Search** across everything.

## What it does that other players can't

Because it talks to M3U Editor directly, M3U TV can use features a generic player never sees. Several arrived alongside the matching features in [M3U Editor 0.12](/blog/m3u-editor-0-12-dvr-arrives):

- **DVR recordings** (1.0.1): watch, cancel, and delete recordings from the TV. See [DVR](/docs/integrations/dvr_integration).
- **AIOStreams** (1.0.2): browse and play your AIOStreams catalogs. See [AIOStreams](/docs/integrations/aiostreams_integration).
- **The proxy and transcoding** (1.0.4): the app follows what each login is allowed to use, and lets you pick a transcoding profile per device.
- **Content requests** (1.0.6): ask for a movie or series, and it goes to Sonarr or Radarr. See [Sonarr and Radarr](/docs/integrations/arrs_integration).
- **Push notifications** (1.0.6): finished syncs, recordings, and alerts on your phone, even with the app closed. They pass through a small open-source relay that stores nothing. See [Push Notifications](/docs/m3u-tv/push-notifications).
- **Trakt**: sync what you watch with your Trakt account.

## Getting connected

Sign in with your server's address and a playlist login, the same one you'd give any Xtream player. M3U Editor needs **Enhanced output enabled** under **Settings → General**, which is on by default.

M3U TV is developed in the open, and a lot of the 1.0 series came from community pull requests. Report bugs and request features on [GitHub](https://github.com/m3ue/m3u-tv).
